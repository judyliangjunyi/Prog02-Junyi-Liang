Voter topics of concern

Bars are a keyword count with no AI: how many paragraphs of the legal text mention each topic's words. A rough signal of where the measure touches your life, not a guarantee of impact. Press Explain for an AI reading of that topic, with its opinion clearly labelled.

Official summary (the state's answer key — never sent to the model)

What each side claims, side by side.

Claims are extracted by an AI model from the printed arguments and rebuttals. This guide never says who is right — it shows where the sides dispute a point, where one side never raised it, and where a point appears only in a rebuttal the other side could not answer.

The races at the bottom of the ballot.

These offices decide real things that almost nobody can describe. Each explanation is generated from the handout's description, and says what the office does not control as well as what it does.
